Ingredient sprites in this directory are CC0 (public domain), sourced from the
"CC0 Food Icons" collection curated by AntumDeluge on OpenGameArt:
https://opengameart.org/content/cc0-food-icons

Contributing sets used here:
- 7Soul1 (32x32 food sprites)
- OpenClipart Library (OCAL) food icons
- Glitch (the game) food art, released as CC0 by Tiny Speck
- thekingphoenix (32x32 food icons)
- bluecarrot16 (24x24 LPC food)
- Jetrel (32x32 food)
- Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup food tiles
- Wikimedia-derived pepper sprites

All licensed Creative Commons Zero (CC0). No attribution required; this file
exists so future maintainers know where replacements can be sourced.

The twelve sprites for staples the set lacked (beans, broth, can, extract,
ginger, lime, sauce, seeds, spirits, spread, vinegar, wine) were made with
ChatGPT image generation, prompted with this set as the style reference, then
cut out, downscaled to 32x32 with a nearest-neighbour fit, and reduced to a
20-colour palette. They are not from the CC0 collection above.
