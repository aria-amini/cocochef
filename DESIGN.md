# cocochef

A recipe app that feels like a kitchen, not software. It's used mid-cooking, so
it stays calm, plain and easy to hit.

- **Paper and ink are the app.** A cream ground, near-black text and hairlines,
  flat surfaces, soft corners, lowercase words. One solid ink button per screen,
  for the thing to do next.
- **Food is the color.** Tomato, yolk, basil and eggplant, as small tints that
  mean something. Never a wash behind a section, never a fifth hue.
- **The food is the charm.** Pixel ingredients and recipe photos carry the
  personality, so the frame stays plain: no decoration of its own.
- **When in doubt, quieter.**

Moved past: neobrutalism, metro tiles, a pantry shelf, a diner ticket rail.
