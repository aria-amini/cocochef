You are drawing pixel-art ingredient icons for a cooking app. Match the attached
reference sheets exactly in style. They show my existing icons enlarged 7x.

RULES FOR EVERY ICON

- Logical size 32x32 pixels per icon, drawn as chunky square pixel blocks (each
  logical pixel is about 16x16 image pixels). No anti-aliasing, no blur, no
  smooth gradients, no dithering, no glow.
- One closed outline, 1 logical pixel wide, in near-black warm brown (about
  #2A1410) around the whole object. No gaps in it. Inner outlines only where two
  parts meet, such as lid and jar.
- Shading: highlight, base and shadow tones per material, with light from the
  upper left. A one-pixel specular glint on glass and metal. Saturated, slightly
  warm colours like the references. About 12 colours per icon.
- Three-quarter front view, upright, centred, filling about 26 of the 32 pixels
  in the tallest direction. No ground shadow, no cast shadow, no floor line.
- Background is flat pure white (#FFFFFF) everywhere outside the outline. Never
  let a white part of the object touch the background without the dark outline
  between them.
- No text, letters, numbers or logos. Labels are blank cream rectangles.

SHEET LAYOUT

- Each sheet gives a grid size. Draw one image: equal square cells, wide white
  gutters, one icon centred in each cell, same scale in every cell, reading left
  to right then top to bottom. No captions or cell numbers in the image.

TINT KEY (important)

- In some icons one part gets recoloured by my app. Paint that part ONLY in
  these four magenta shades: light #FF9AFF, base #FF00FF, shade #A800A8, deep
  #5A005A Use them with the same light logic as any other colour: base for most
  of the area, light for the top-left edge, shade and deep for the lower-right
  and the inside edges.
- Magenta, pink and purple must not appear anywhere else on that icon.
  Everything that does not change colour (outline, lid, cork, cap, rim, label,
  glints) uses normal colours.
- Icons that have no recolourable part contain no magenta at all.
