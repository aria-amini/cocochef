Using the style rules from the master prompt and the attached reference sheets,
draw ONE image with a grid of 4 columns x 2 rows (8 equal square cells, wide

5. spice-pouch: Kraft paper stand-up pouch with a zip top and a round clear

6. salt-shaker: Classic hourglass-shaped glass salt shaker with a chrome cap

7. pepper-mill: Wooden pepper mill in dark wood with a metal crown and knob on
