Sheet 5: Herbs.

Using the style rules from the master prompt and the attached reference sheets,
draw ONE image with a grid of 4 columns x 2 rows (8 equal square cells, wide
white gutters, one icon centred per cell, same scale throughout, reading left to
right then top to bottom). Icons:

1. herb-bunch: Tied bunch of flat-leaf herbs (parsley or cilantro) with stems at

   stems and string stay normal.
2. round-leaf-sprig: One stem with small round paired leaves, like thyme or
   oregano. RECOLOURABLE (magenta): the leaves; the stem stays normal.
3. basil: Four broad oval leaves on one short stem, like basil or mint.
   RECOLOURABLE (magenta): the leaves; the stem stays normal.
4. rosemary: Woody sprig covered in thin needle leaves. RECOLOURABLE (magenta):
   the needles; the woody stem stays normal.
5. dill: Feathery dill fronds on a thin stem. RECOLOURABLE (magenta): the
   fronds; the stem stays normal.
6. chives: Small bundle of thin hollow chive stalks tied once, cut ends at the
   bottom. RECOLOURABLE (magenta): the stalks.
7. bay-leaves: Three dried bay leaves, olive-green going to brown, one curled.
   No magenta; all normal colours.
8. lemongrass: Two trimmed lemongrass stalks with pale bulbs and green tops. No
   magenta; all normal colours.

Pure white background, no captions, no numbers, no text in the image.
